PARTICIPATION AND ACCESS REGULATION FOR

"PLANETA CRĂCIUN" CHRISTMAS FAIR

Unirii Square in Cluj-Napoca

  1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

    1. The purpose of the "Participation and Access Regulation", hereinafter referred to as the Regulation, is to define the terms and conditions of the Christmas Fair "PLANET CHRISTMAS", hereinafter referred to as the Christmas Fair, organized in the bounded area of the Unirii Square in Cluj-Napoca, hereinafter referred to as the Christmas Fair Development Area.

    2. The event is organized by Fantesia Untold S.R.L., a Romanian legal entity with registered office in Cluj-Napoca, Str. Eremia Grigorescu, no.122A, Cluj County, entered at the Trade Register Office of the Cluj Court under no. J12/2972/2021, Tax Identification Code RO44485633.

    3. Access to the event area is free, within the limit of the available capacity. The organizer reserves the right to stop access to the event area, for safety reasons, if the maximum capacity has been reached.

    4. This Regulation is displayed and can be consulted at the entrance to the Christmas Fair and in the event area.

    5. In accordance with changes to the national or local legislation on which this Regulation is based, they will be updated whenever necessary. Furthermore, the Organizer reserves the right to unilaterally amend the content of this Regulation, with the amendments coming into force after posting.

    6. By entering the Christmas Fair venue, the participants or authorized persons agree that they have read in full and understood the content of the Regulation, and undertake to comply with its provisions.

II. APPLICABILITY

2.1. This Regulation is addressed to:

  1. Christmas Fair Participants;

  2. Authorized persons;

III. RUNNING PERIOD

The Christmas Market period means the interval between the official opening of the Market (20.11.2026) and the closing of the Market (01.01.2027), during which participants and authorized persons have access to the Christmas Market schedule, in compliance with these Regulations.
The Christmas Market schedule is as follows:

  • Monday - Thursday: 10:00 - 22:00

  • Friday - Sunday: 10:00 - 22:00

  • December 24: 10:00 - 16:00

  • December 25: 14:00 - 22:00

  • December 31: 14:00 - 01:00

Between 20.11.2026 and 31.12.2026, the schedule of Santa’s House is as follows:

  • Monday - Thursday: 14:00 - 22:00

  • Friday - Sunday: 12:00 - 22:00

During 20.11.2026 - 23.12.2026, workshops for children are organized between the following hours:

  • Monday - Thursday: 18:00 - 20:00

  • Friday - Sunday: 16:00 - 19:00

The detailed schedule is announced on site.

IV. SAFETY AND SECURITY

4.1. The Organizer reserves the right to subject the public to body checks prior to entering the event premises, and all bags, backpacks, handbags and packages will be checked in accordance with the law by legally authorized security personnel.

4.2. The organizer reserves the right not to allow access to the event to those who refuse security checks or to persons who are suspected of endangering other participants or the smooth running of the event.

4.3. The public is obliged to respect both these Regulations and other Regulations relating to the activities during the event.

4.4. It is strictly forbidden for participants to enter with:

  • toxic, flammable or explosive materials, paralyzing sprays and pyrotechnic articles;

  • Lasers, banners, flags, vuvuzelas;

  • weapons, knives, chains or other blunt/cold steel, prohibited substances, drugs;

  • drones or other products and equipment that can be hoisted over participants. ;

  • Bicycles, scooters, longboards, skateboards, rollerblades, roller skates, segways, unicycles, hoverboards, etc.;

  • water, soft drinks, alcoholic beverages, glass or plastic containers, food, canned goods, etc.; flyers, samples and other promotional products for the purpose of distribution at the Christmas Fair;

  • fireworks, firecrackers, clacks and other pyrotechnics;

4.5. In front of the stage, the noise produced can be much louder than the level the little ones can bear, therefore the organizer recommends the use of special headphones to protect children's hearing.

4.6. During the Festival, there will be various high-intensity light shows and pyrotechnics which may be harmful to children or people with epilepsy.

4.7. Participants who suffer from mental or physical illness and who may be affected in any way by loud noises, special visual effects, crowded areas, special sound effects or any other such effects, assume full responsibility for any injury they may suffer.

4.8. For situations requiring medical assistance, the event shall provide emergency interventions with dedicated medical teams on site.

4.9. Access by persons under the influence of alcohol/illegal substances is strictly forbidden.

4.10. The organizer reserves the right to evict from the Christmas Fair, in compliance with the legal provisions, participants who do not comply with the requests of the organizer or the security company regarding the smooth running of the Christmas Fair. If the situation so requires, they will be handed over to the competent authorities.

4.11. The participants are obliged to notify the organizer or the security agents of any irregularities they report during their participation in the Christmas Fair.

4.12. The entire area of the Christmas Fair is under the surveillance of video cameras by the company providing the security services. By their presence at the Christmas Fair, participants consent to being under video surveillance for the entire period of their stay at the venue.

V. CONDUCT DURING THE EVENT

5.1. It is forbidden to sell products and to distribute promotional materials or any other form of advertising both on the premises of the Christmas Fair and in the vicinity of the event without a written consent from the organizer.

5.2. The organizer supports the promotion of traditional customs and traditions, but for the safety of the participants, spontaneous artistic activities are forbidden without the written consent of the organizer.

5.3. The organizer has the right to stop spontaneous artistic and other spontaneous activities, individually/collectively carried out, which may endanger the safety of the initiators or other participants, even if they have been agreed beforehand.

5.4. Visitors have the obligation to refrain from any actions, statements or behavior that may endanger the life, health or physical integrity of others or that may violate their rights.

5.5. Participants are obliged to supervise their personal belongings throughout their participation in the Christmas Fair; the organizer is not responsible for their loss, destruction or theft.

5.6. Hot food products are sold at the Christmas Fair. The organizer recommends handling them properly in order to avoid possible injuries.

5.7. Participation in the Christmas Fair is at the person's own risk and persons must ensure that they are physically and mentally fit when exposed to the following situations/activities:

  • Presence in possible crowded areas with loud noises, visual or audio special effects;

  • entertainment activities with risk of injury.

*** Minors and their parents/guardian/caregiver are solely responsible for the risks to which they are exposed by participating in the Christmas Fair and their involvement in incidents resulting in physical/psychic health impairment or injury caused by them.

5.8 We recommend that adults accompany children under 14 years of age on the perimeter of the event, as they are directly responsible for the safety of minors.

VI. SALE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES

6.1. The sale of alcoholic beverages to persons under 18 years of age is prohibited by law. Also, the consumption of alcoholic beverages is prohibited to this category of persons, according to the law. We recommend moderate alcohol consumption. Merchants on the perimeter of the Christmas Fair can ask for an ID to verify the age of consumers.

VII. ACCESS WITH PETS

7.1. The organizer recommends that participants do not bring pets to the event, as a very large number of participants is expected and the noise produced may be much more than the level they can tolerate.

7.2. However, if the participant wishes to bring a pet, it must be kept on a leash and large pets must also be muzzled (pet owners are solely responsible for the actions of their animals). Dog owners are required to clean up after their pets.

VIII. CLEANING

8.1 In order to maintain cleanliness in the Christmas Fair area, please dispose of garbage and household debris in the bins located on site.

XI. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

9.1 Everything that is posted on various types of media in the Fair Area, such as, but not limited to, images, text, graphics, symbols, logos, databases, etc. is the property of the Organizer and/or its Contractual Partners. All this is subject to intellectual property law.

9.2. Visitors and Third Parties may not use, copy, distribute, publish or incorporate into other documents or materials such markings/information in any form for the purpose of obtaining revenue without the express prior written and express permission of the Organizer and/or its Contractual Partners.

X. PHOTO/VIDEO

10.1 During the Christmas Fair, the Organizer and accredited persons have the right to record/photograph the Christmas Fair. By being present at the Christmas Fair, members of the public give their consent to appear in the photographs and recordings taken and their subsequent use by the Organizer for advertising, promotional and documentation purposes of the event. Access to the Christmas Fair with cameras and camcorders is allowed.

10.2 By attending this event, you acknowledge that images or recordings that include you may be used publicly and may appear in media materials associated with the event, including, but not limited to, the event website, social media, print materials and other communication channels.

10.3 If you are the parent or guardian of a minor participant, by allowing the minor's participation in the event, you grant permission for the photographing and use of audio-visual materials associated with the minor.

10.4 The Organizer and authorized persons have the unrestricted right to profit from, use (in particular for the purpose of promoting the Festival), copy, publish, publicly broadcast and distribute such recordings of the Visitors, without having to pay the Visitors for it.

10.5. The Event Organizers undertake to respect the privacy and dignity of Participants. If you have any particular concerns or requests regarding the use of your images, please contact us in advance.

10.6 The Organizer reserves the right to establish areas/locations with prohibited photography/filming. In the event of non-compliance, the Organizer has the right to delete such recordings.

XI. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 The Organizer is not liable for theft, loss or destruction of personal property or force majeure.

11.2. The Organizer shall not be liable for the Participants' property or any damage caused to it.

11.3. The Organizer does not guarantee the possibility to enter the Fair at the desired time or the possibility to attend the desired concert on time or the quality of the entertainment, and excludes the payment of damages, indemnities or compensation of any kind to the Visitor.

11.4. During the Fair, the Organizer may unilaterally make changes to the program (artistic, organizational, etc.), depending on various situations that may arise during the Fair.

11.5. The Organizer reserves the right to modify, restructure and develop the Services and Products offered at the Fair, at its own discretion, according to its needs. Visitors are not entitled to make any claims against the Organizer in respect of such changes, restructuring or further development.

11.6. The Organizer is under no obligation to cancel the Fair in the event of bad weather, however, in the event that the authorities request the suspension or closure of the Fair by the Organizer due to unforeseeable circumstances or force majeure, the Fair or any part thereof shall be cancelled/suspended and the Organizer cannot be held liable to the visitors or other third parties for its closure/suspension.

11.7. Visitors expressly note that they may also purchase Products and Services in connection with the Fair that are offered by other Contractual Partners and not by the Organizer. In such cases, the contract is concluded directly between the Visitor and the Contractual Partner, and the rights and obligations arising from the legal relationship apply exclusively to the Visitor and the Contractual Partner.

11.8. Visitors may enter the Fair and participate in the various activities provided exclusively at their own risk. The Organizer shall be liable exclusively for intentional breaches of contract directly attributable to the Organizer and breaches of contract endangering human life, physical integrity or health, if such damage is caused exclusively by the Organizer and could not have been avoided by the Participant by taking reasonable care to do so, and excludes any liability beyond the express legal rights of the Participants in connection with any damage, including damage to human life, physical integrity or health or damage to property.

11.9. The Organizer shall not be liable for the unlawful activities or omissions of any Visitor, Unauthorized Participant, pet or Third Party. If a Visitor, Unauthorized Participant, Third Party causes damage of any kind to another Visitor, Unauthorized Participant or Third Party, the latter must take any action against the person at fault to repair the damage and not against the Organizer.

11.10. The Organizer also assumes no liability for any damage or injury that may arise from fireworks. Participants assume full responsibility for their physical/psychic ability to participate in the various activities of the Event.

XII. DISPUTES

12.1. Any dispute arising between the Organizer and visitors as a result of their participation in the Fair will be settled amicably. In case this is not possible, the resolution of disputes shall be the competence of the Romanian courts in Cluj-Napoca.

12.2. The applicable law is Romanian law.

Form of the Participation and Access Regulation updated on the 11th of November 2026.
